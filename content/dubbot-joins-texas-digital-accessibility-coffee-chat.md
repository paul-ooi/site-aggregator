DubBot is pleased to join the [Texas Department of Information Resources](https://dir.texas.gov/ "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") for its upcoming [Texas Digital Accessibility Coffee Chat](https://dir.texas.gov/events/texas-digital-accessibility-coffee-chat-7 "This link leaves the DubBlog website."), a monthly meetup designed for Texas public-sector employees only who work to improve digital accessibility.

The Coffee Chats also feature guest speakers from vendor partners who specialize in accessible technology products and services. As part of that community, [Penny Kronz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/penny-kronz-7046969/ "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") of DubBot will present _Ensuring Website Accessibility through Automated and Manual Testing_, sharing practical insights into how automated and manual testing can work together to identify and address accessibility issues.

We’re excited to be part of the conversation on Tuesday, September 8 at 9:30 a.m. CDT and connect with those who are working to make digital content more accessible.

We hope to see you there, and, of course, coffee is encouraged. ☕
