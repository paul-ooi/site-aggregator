DubBot is excited to return as a platinum sponsor of [Digital Collegium’s Accessibility Summit](https://events.digicol.org/a11ysummit26/ "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") on Tuesday, July 28, 2026.

This [one-day, multi-track virtual event](https://events.digicol.org/a11ysummit26/schedule) brings together higher education professionals focused on advancing digital accessibility through strategy, design, and technical execution. It’s a space for practical insights and real-world conversations about what it takes to build more inclusive digital user experiences.

If you're just beginning your accessibility journey or looking to strengthen your testing skills, [Penny Kronz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/penny-kronz-7046969/), DubBot’s VP of Client Services, has just what you need. In her presentation, [_New to Accessibility Testing? A Straightforward Review of Accessibility Testing Basics_](https://events.digicol.org/a11ysummit26/session/4242027/new-to-accessibility-testing-a-straightforward-review-of-accessibility-testing-basics), Penny will break down the [fundamentals of WCAG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) in a practical, approachable way and share essential testing techniques you and your team can put into practice right away.

So whether you're laying the groundwork or refining a mature accessibility program, the Accessibility Summit offers sessions designed to meet you where you are and push your work forward. Join peers from institutions across the country to exchange ideas, tackle shared challenges, and move accessibility from obligation to impact.

[**Registration is open now**](https://events.digicol.org/a11ysummit26/registration "This link leaves the DubBlog website.")! 

We look forward to **_seeing_** **👀** you in July!
