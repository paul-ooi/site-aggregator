May always brings a bit of extra energy to the accessibility world, and DubBot is leaning all the way in. Between [Global Accessibility Awareness Day](https://accessibility.day/ "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") and the wider push for digital inclusion, it’s a reminder that accessibility is an ongoing journey, not a destination.

At DubBot, we’re celebrating what we call **A11Y Love**! That steady commitment to digital accessibility, ongoing compliance, and a better, more thoughtful user experience for everyone.

And yes, we’re making it a little more fun!

Because advocacy deserves a bit of swag, we’re sharing our **A11Y Love sticker**. It’s our way of celebrating your commitment to building a more inclusive web.

If you’re a DubBot client, partner, or just someone who cares deeply about accessibility, we’re happy to send a little A11Y Love your way.

Grab a few and decorate your laptop, water bottle, notebook, or anywhere you want to show your support for a more inclusive web.

Think of it as a low-tech reminder of a high-impact idea - accessibility is everyone’s responsibility!
