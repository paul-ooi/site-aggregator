DubBot is excited to once again join the DigiCol community as a Platinum Sponsor and Accessibility Partner for the [2026 Digital Collegium Annual Conference](https://events.digicol.org/digicol26 "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") in [Pittsburgh, PA](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/things-to-do/ "This link leaves the DubBlog website.").

Join us in person or online for DigiCol 2026, happening October 18 - 21. This event brings together content creators, strategists, designers, and developers of higher ed to connect, share, and learn from one another. **This is** the _premier gathering for higher education professionals passionate about digital innovation._

Whether it’s content strategy, UX, accessibility, AI, or the day-to-day realities of managing digital systems in higher ed, DigiCol has always been about community. A space where people come together to share ideas, collaborate, and learn.

Be sure to attend [Penny Kronz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/penny-kronz-7046969/ "This link leaves the DubBlog website.")’s session, [_Ensuring Website Accessibility through Automated and Manual Testing_](https://events.digicol.org/digicol26/session/4528617/ensuring-website-accessibility-through-automated-and-manual-testing "This link leaves the DubBlog website."), where she’ll share practical insights into how automated and manual testing work together to identify and address accessibility issues.

And when the workday is done, we’ll be trading accessibility checks for microphones! And we’re pretty excited about it!

DubBot is teaming up with our friends at [Evolving Web](https://evolvingweb.com/?utm_medium=email&utm_source=digicol26&utm_campaign=sponsor_dubbot "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") to sponsor the [Karaokeplane](https://events.digicol.org/digicol26/session/4500443/karaokeplane-2026%C2%A0-%E2%80%94-sponsored-by-dubbot-and-evolving-web?utm_medium=email&utm_source=digicol26&utm_campaign=sponsor_dubbot "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") on Tuesday evening at [Tequila Cowboy](https://tequilacowboy.com/pittsburgh/?utm_medium=email&utm_source=digicol26&utm_campaign=sponsor_dubbot "This link leaves the DubBlog website.").

Nothing brings the higher ed digital community together like swapping website governance stories for microphones, turning up the music, and seeing who’s ready to take the stage. 

We’ll see yinz in Pittsburgh!
