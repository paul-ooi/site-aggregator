Every organization manages its websites, page sets, and task responsibilities in different ways. You could manage sites by department, audience, campus, or business unit. You may want to distinguish tasks by status, priority, or workflow.

Now, DubBot lets you apply that same approach to your organization’s structure and workflows right in the app with **Labels**.

[Labels are customizable tags](https://help.dubbot.com/en/articles/17031640-labels-now-available-in-dubbot "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") that help you organize and filter Sites, Page Sets, and Tasks in ways that make sense for your organization. Account-level Administrator and Manager roles can create labels, giving your organization the flexibility to build a system around existing workflows rather than forcing workflows into a predefined structure.

![Dark blue arrow pointing to different Labels. One label is blue with black text of Content Marketing. The other Label is red with white text of Weekly Edits.](../../images/blog-content/2026/here-is-a-labelv2.png)

## Make It Easier to Find What You Need

Labels help you quickly find and focus on what you need.

As your digital presence grows, finding the right information quickly becomes as important as having it in the first place. Labels give you a simple way to narrow your scope and focus on what matters.

For example, you can [create Site or Page Set labels](https://help.dubbot.com/en/articles/17060294-using-labels-with-sites-and-page-sets "This link leaves the DubBlog website.") based on _department, audience, or organizational area_. Once you apply those labels, you can filter listings and dashboards to locate a specific group of sites or page sets. 

[Labels can also add more structure to Tasks](https://help.dubbot.com/en/articles/17061408-using-labels-with-tasks "This link leaves the DubBlog website."). Use them to track workflow stages such as _To Do, In Progress, and Resolved_. You can assign one or more labels to Tasks and filter by those labels when you need to zero in on a particular set of work.

**Note:** All Labels are visible to all account holders. Creating and managing labels requires an Administrator or Manager role. Labels are also available on applicable CSV downloads.

## Build a System That Fits Your Workflow

[Creating a label is straightforward](https://help.dubbot.com/en/articles/17033020-create-labels "This link leaves the DubBlog website."). Choose whether the label applies to Sites, Page Sets, or Tasks, then give it a name, description, and background color. Because labels are specific to each object type, you can create a labeling system that reflects your organization's structure and workflow. 

And that's the idea. Labels let you bring that structure into DubBot, making it easier to find, filter, and manage the work that matters to you.
